About the project

BlazePlus started with a simple question: how does a growing team keep track of who's who, without yet another spreadsheet floating around? I built it from the ground up in raw PHP and MySQL, handling everything from the sign-up flow to the admin tools that keep the whole directory in order.

It's not just a database with a login screen, though. The part I spent the most time on was the stuff in between — getting someone into the platform safely, letting them talk to their team without friction, and giving admins a way to keep things running without babysitting every action. I documented the build daily on LinkedIn as it came together.

What it does

Getting people in the door

New hires don't just type in an email and get access. They verify their account, then an admin gives the final sign-off before anything opens up.

Sign up→Verify→Admin review→Access

Not everyone needs to see everything

What you can view depends on your role. A team member sees their own department; an admin sees the full picture. Nobody's digging through information that isn't theirs to see.

A place to actually talk

Each department gets its own live chat room, updating in real time through AJAX polling, with support for sharing images and PDFs — no refreshing, no waiting around.

Moving desks without the paperwork

Changing departments doesn't mean emailing IT and hoping someone gets to it. People can request the move themselves, right from their profile.

Reaching out without oversharing

Contact details aren't just public to everyone. People send a request first — a small buffer that keeps things respectful across roles.

Keeping the place tidy

Admins work from a dashboard with three separate review queues and moderation tools, so nothing odd slips through unnoticed.
